Guangzhou’s Best Family-Friendly Ice Skating Rinks

Home / Travel Blog / Blog Details

Guangzhou, the vibrant capital of Guangdong Province, is known for its subtropical heat, bustling markets, and rich culinary scene. But what many travelers don’t realize is that this southern metropolis also offers a surprising winter wonderland for families looking to beat the heat. Ice skating in Guangzhou has become a major tourism trend, especially as parents seek unique indoor activities that combine fun, fitness, and a touch of frosty magic. Whether you’re a local or a tourist visiting with kids, the city’s ice rinks provide a refreshing escape from the humidity, blending modern entertainment with family-friendly amenities. In this guide, we’ll explore the best ice skating rinks in Guangzhou, focusing on what makes each venue perfect for families, along with tips on nearby attractions, dining, and how to make the most of your cool adventure.

Why Ice Skating is a Hot Trend for Guangzhou Families

Guangzhou’s climate, with its long summers and mild winters, makes indoor ice skating a year-round attraction. For families, it’s more than just a sport—it’s a social experience that encourages bonding, physical activity, and a break from screen time. The rise of family tourism in Guangzhou has led to a boom in entertainment complexes that combine shopping, dining, and skating under one roof. Many of these rinks are located in popular malls or tourist hubs, making them easy to access via the city’s efficient metro system. Additionally, ice skating offers a unique cultural contrast: while Guangzhou is famous for its Cantonese opera and dim sum, stepping onto the ice feels like entering a different world, one that kids find thrilling and adults find nostalgic.

The Top Family-Friendly Ice Skating Rinks in Guangzhou

1. Grand View Ice Skating Rink (Da Guan Bing Chang) – A Classic Choice in Tianhe

Located in the heart of Tianhe District, the Grand View Ice Skating Rink is one of Guangzhou’s oldest and most beloved indoor rinks. Housed within the Grand View Plaza shopping mall, this rink has been a favorite for families since it opened. The rink itself is Olympic-sized, offering plenty of space for beginners and advanced skaters alike. What sets it apart for families is its dedicated “Penguin Zone,” where young children can use plastic penguin-shaped skating aids to build confidence on the ice. The staff is known for being patient and helpful, often offering free mini-lessons for first-timers.

Why families love it: The rink has a warm, welcoming atmosphere, and the mall surrounding it is packed with kid-friendly restaurants and toy stores. After skating, families can grab a meal at the food court, which features everything from Cantonese roast duck to Western-style pasta. The rink also hosts regular “Family Night” events, where admission is discounted for groups, and there’s even a small arcade area for kids who need a break from the ice.

Nearby attractions: Tianhe is a major commercial and cultural hub. After your skating session, take a short metro ride to the Canton Tower, where you can enjoy panoramic views of the city. The Guangdong Museum is also nearby, offering interactive exhibits that teach kids about local history and nature.

2. ICE Town – The Ultimate Indoor Snow Experience in Panyu

If your family is looking for something beyond a standard skating rink, ICE Town in Panyu District is a must-visit. This massive indoor snow park combines a real ice rink with a snow play area, making it feel like a winter resort in the middle of Guangzhou. ICE Town is part of the larger Guangzhou Snow World complex, which includes a ski slope, snow tubing lanes, and a igloo-themed café. The main rink is large and well-maintained, with rental skates that are regularly sanitized—a big plus for hygiene-conscious parents.

Why families love it: ICE Town is designed for all ages. Toddlers can toddle on the ice with the help of “seal” walkers, while older kids and parents can practice spins and jumps. The snow play area is a huge hit, featuring artificial snow that’s soft and safe for building snowmen or having snowball fights. The complex also offers skate sharpening services and a cozy lounge where parents can watch their kids from a heated glass enclosure.

Nearby attractions: Panyu is home to the famous Chimelong Tourist Resort, which includes Chimelong Paradise (a theme park with roller coasters) and Chimelong Safari Park. Many families combine a day at ICE Town with a visit to the safari, making it a full weekend getaway. For a quieter option, explore the ancient Chen Clan Ancestral Hall in the city center, which showcases traditional Lingnan architecture.

3. The Rink at TaiKoo Hui – Luxury and Convenience in the CBD

For families who want to combine skating with high-end shopping and dining, the rink at TaiKoo Hui is unbeatable. Located in the upscale TaiKoo Hui mall in Tianhe, this rink is smaller than Grand View but offers a more polished experience. The ice is kept at a pristine level, and the lighting system creates a magical atmosphere, especially during evening sessions. The rink is known for its professional coaches, many of whom have competed internationally, making it a great place for kids who want to take skating seriously.

Why families love it: TaiKoo Hui is a one-stop destination. While kids skate, parents can browse luxury boutiques like Gucci and Prada, or relax at the mall’s rooftop garden. The rink offers “Family Skate Passes” that include skate rental and a drink voucher for the café next door. The café serves kid-friendly snacks like hot chocolate and mini pizzas, and there’s a viewing area with comfortable seating for parents who prefer to watch from the sidelines.

Nearby attractions: TaiKoo Hui is connected to the Guangzhou Opera House, a stunning architectural landmark designed by Zaha Hadid. Families can catch a kid-friendly performance or simply admire the building’s futuristic design. The nearby Guangdong Provincial Library also has a dedicated children’s section with story time sessions.

4. Happy Ice Rink – Budget-Friendly Fun in Haizhu

If you’re traveling on a budget but still want quality ice time, Happy Ice Rink in Haizhu District is a great option. This rink is located in a community sports center, so it has a more local, less touristy vibe. The rink is smaller but well-maintained, and the staff is incredibly friendly. Happy Ice Rink is known for its affordable pricing, with weekday sessions costing as little as 30 RMB per person. They also offer monthly membership cards for families who plan to visit multiple times.

Why families love it: The rink has a “Kids First” policy, meaning that during certain hours, the ice is reserved exclusively for children under 12. This makes it a safe space for beginners to practice without worrying about faster skaters. The rink also provides free helmets and knee pads, which is a nice touch for parents who forgot to bring their own. After skating, families can explore the nearby Haizhu Wetland Park, a peaceful nature reserve with walking trails and bird-watching opportunities.

Nearby attractions: Haizhu is known for its authentic Cantonese street food. After your skating session, visit the Shangxiajiu Pedestrian Street for a bowl of wonton noodles or steamed rice rolls. The area is also home to the Sun Yat-sen University campus, which has beautiful gardens and a museum that’s free to enter.

5. Ice World at Wanda Plaza – A Complete Entertainment Hub in Baiyun

Wanda Plaza in Baiyun District is a massive commercial complex that includes a cinema, a bowling alley, and, of course, an ice skating rink. Ice World is the centerpiece of the plaza, offering a spacious rink that’s popular with both locals and tourists. The rink features a state-of-the-art sound system and colorful LED lights that create a party-like atmosphere, especially during weekend sessions. For families, the rink offers “Parent-Child Skate Sessions” where parents and kids can learn together with a dedicated instructor.

Why families love it: Wanda Plaza is a destination in itself. The mall has a huge indoor playground for younger kids, a karaoke bar for older teens, and dozens of restaurants serving cuisines from around the world. The rink also hosts birthday parties, with packages that include private skating time, a cake, and party favors. It’s a popular choice for families celebrating a child’s birthday while on vacation.

Nearby attractions: Baiyun District is home to Baiyun Mountain, a scenic area with hiking trails and a cable car. Families can spend the morning hiking, then cool off with an afternoon of skating. The area also has several hot spring resorts, which are a relaxing way to end a day of activity.

Practical Tips for Families Visiting Guangzhou’s Ice Rinks

Choosing the Right Time to Go

Weekday mornings are the best time for families with young children, as the rinks are less crowded and often have lower prices. Many rinks offer “Mommy and Me” sessions during these hours, where parents can skate with toddlers in a calm environment. Weekend afternoons are busier but also more lively, with music and events that older kids might enjoy. Avoid evening sessions on weekends if your family prefers a quieter experience.

What to Bring and Wear

Most rinks provide skate rentals, but it’s a good idea to bring your own socks, as rental socks can be thin and uncomfortable. Dress in layers: a light jacket or sweater over a T-shirt is usually enough, as the rinks are kept at around 10°C (50°F). Gloves are highly recommended, both for warmth and to protect hands from ice burns. For toddlers, consider bringing a small cushion or padded shorts, as falls are common.

Safety First

All the rinks listed above have safety barriers and first-aid stations. However, parents should always supervise young children, especially near the entrance and exit points. Many rinks provide helmets for free, so don’t hesitate to ask. If your child is nervous, start with a penguin or seal skating aid, which most rinks offer for a small fee.

Combining Skating with Other Activities

Guangzhou is a city of contrasts, and ice skating is just one piece of the puzzle. Many families plan their day around the rink’s location. For example, after skating at Grand View, you can walk to the nearby Tianhe Sports Center for a game of badminton or swimming. After ICE Town, head to the Chimelong Safari Park for a wildlife adventure. The key is to choose a rink that fits your family’s overall itinerary.

The Growing Trend of Ice Skating Tourism in Guangzhou

Ice skating in Guangzhou is more than just a pastime—it’s a reflection of the city’s evolving tourism landscape. As Guangzhou attracts more international visitors, the demand for family-friendly indoor activities has surged. The city’s rinks are now competing with each other to offer unique experiences, from themed nights to seasonal festivals. For instance, during Chinese New Year, many rinks decorate with red lanterns and host lion dance performances on ice. During Christmas, they transform into winter wonderlands with fake snow and Santa Claus meet-and-greets. These events draw crowds from across the region, making ice skating a key part of Guangzhou’s tourist appeal.

Moreover, the rise of social media has turned ice skating into a visual spectacle. Parents love capturing their kids’ first skate on video, and the rinks’ colorful lighting and clean aesthetics make for perfect Instagram posts. Some rinks even offer professional photography services, allowing families to take home high-quality memories.

Final Thoughts on Guangzhou’s Ice Skating Scene

Guangzhou’s family-friendly ice skating rinks offer a unique way to experience the city beyond its famous dim sum and Cantonese opera. Whether you choose the classic charm of Grand View, the snowy extravagance of ICE Town, or the luxury of TaiKoo Hui, each venue provides a safe, fun, and memorable outing for families. The city’s commitment to blending modern entertainment with traditional hospitality ensures that every skating session feels special. So next time you’re in Guangzhou, lace up your skates, hold your child’s hand, and glide into a cool adventure that you’ll all remember for years to come.
